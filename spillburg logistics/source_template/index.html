About Spillburg Logistics 1

SL1 // PROFILE

Spillburg Logistics 1 (Private) Limited is a premier Logistic Service Provider and licensed clearing agent with a team of seasoned experts providing international transport solutions for customers around the globe. Headquartered in Colombo, we deliver reliable cargo handling services for both export and import consignments at Sri Lanka Customs.

Ensuring that cargo arrives at the right place and at the right time involves real skill. Spillburg Logistics 1 ensures that moving goods from one part of the world to another is executed economically and efficiently, following strict statutory procedures and accurate shipping documentation to facilitate every transaction seamlessly.

Fast & Professional Execution

Moving finished products, raw materials, and industrial equipment worldwide requires specialized operational expertise. Businesses across Sri Lanka and international trade corridors rely on Spillburg Logistics 1 for experienced, responsive, and high-efficiency freight forwarding across ocean, air, and road networks.

DO collection, terminal gate-out, inland haulage & signed Proof of Delivery (POD).

Regional Trade Agreement Advisory (SAFTA / ISLFTA / SLPFTA)

Led by our Managing Director’s extensive background with the Board of Investment (BOI) and regional trade treaties, we assist exporters and importers in structuring shipments under the Indo-Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement (ISLFTA), Pakistan-Sri Lanka FTA (SLPFTA), and SAFTA—securing duty concessions and rules-of-origin compliance.

Multi-Country Consolidation (MCC) & Entrepôt Trade

We coordinate LCL cargo originating from multiple regional ports to be de-stuffed, sorted, relabeled, and consolidated into FCL containers under customs bond in Colombo for onward shipment to Europe, the UK, the Middle East, and North America—reducing overall landed freight costs.

Direct Terminal & Border Coverage Across Sri Lanka

Port of Colombo Terminals

Direct wharf operations across JCT (Jaya Container Terminal), SAGT (South Asia Gateway Terminals), CICT (Colombo International Container Terminals), and ECT.

Hambantota & Trincomalee

Ro-Ro vehicle transshipment, project breakbulk discharge, bulk industrial commodities, and heavy-lift port clearance at regional deep-water ports.

BIA Air Cargo & BOI Zones

Express air cargo clearance at Katunayake (CMB) and direct bonded trucking to Katunayake, Biyagama, Seethawaka, Horana, and Koggala EPZs.
