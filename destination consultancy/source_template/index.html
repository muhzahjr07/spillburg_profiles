Corporate Overview • Who We Are

A Multidisciplinary Advisory Partner

Destination Consultancy (Private) Limited is a premier, Sri Lanka-based multidisciplinary management and investment consultancy firm headquartered in the heart of Colombo. Positioned at the crossroads of South Asian commerce, we serve as a strategic bridge and execution partner for international investors, global corporations, development agencies, and ambitious domestic enterprises looking to establish, scale, or transform their operations in Sri Lanka and across the region.

Whether you are a foreign investor evaluating market entry under the Board of Investment (BOI) or Colombo Port City frameworks, an industrial leader aligning with international ISO and ESG standards, or a fast-growing business building a BPO centre or digital growth engine, Destination Consultancy provides integrated, end-to-end solutions under one roof.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted strategic advisory and execution partner for sustainable investment, enterprise transformation, and cross-border business growth in Sri Lanka and the region.

Our Mission

To empower investors and enterprises with actionable intelligence, seamless regulatory navigation, sustainable frameworks, and end-to-end operational support—turning complex market opportunities into lasting commercial success.

Institutional Navigation • Regulatory Clearances

Investor Facilitation & Regulatory Gateway

Foreign and domestic investments in Sri Lanka require structured coordination across statutory agencies. Destination Consultancy manages the entire regulatory pathway—from project application to commercial licensing—eliminating administrative bottlenecks.

BOI

Board of Investment Approvals

Full lifecycle representation under Section 17 (special incentive regime) and Section 16 (normal law foreign investment entry) of the BOI Act.

Investment application preparation, financial projections & submission
Negotiation & execution of BOI Investment Agreements
Customs duty-free import clearance for project capital goods
Expatriate entry permits & residence visa recommendations
CPCC

Colombo Port City Advisory

Advisory for international businesses establishing offshore entities inside the Colombo Port City Special Economic Zone.

Authorised Person (AP) licensing & offshore company registration
Business of Strategic Importance (BSI) incentive structuring
Designated foreign-currency banking & capital mobility setup
Leasehold plot / commercial office space coordination
CBSL

Foreign Exchange & Banking

Compliant capital inflow structuring under the Foreign Exchange Act and Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) directions.

Inward Investment Account (IIA) opening with licensed banks
Equity remittance, shareholder loan & repatriation structuring
ROC eBiz company incorporation & corporate secretarial setup
IRD tax registration (TIN, Corporate Income Tax, VAT, SVAT)
LINE

Line-Ministry & Sector Approvals

Multi-agency environmental, municipal, and sector-specific approvals required for physical project execution.

Central Environmental Authority (CEA) IEE / EIA & EPL clearances
Urban Development Authority (UDA) & local authority permits
Sri Lanka Sustainable Energy Authority (SLSEA) & CEB approvals
Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) licensing

Single-Window Execution Advantage: Rather than managing separate legal, engineering, environmental, and corporate consultants, investors rely on Destination Consultancy as a unified project management office (PMO) that coordinates every regulatory milestone from day one.
