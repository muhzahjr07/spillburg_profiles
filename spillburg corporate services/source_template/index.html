The Spillburg Story • Who We Are

One-Stop-Shop Corporate Solutions

01

Architects of Corporate Excellence in Sri Lanka

Founded over 15 years ago, Spillburg Corporate Services (Private) Limited was built on a singular premise: businesses expanding into or operating within Sri Lanka deserve a single, reliable partner capable of handling complex regulatory, financial, secretarial, and operational mandates under one roof.

We have evolved from a boutique consultancy into a full-scale corporate services powerhouse. By combining deep local regulatory intelligence across the Registrar of Companies (ROC), Board of Investment (BOI), Inland Revenue Department (IRD), Central Bank (CBSL), and Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) with global best practices, we ensure absolute compliance and financial precision.

Our Mission

To empower global and local enterprises with seamless, accurate, and compliant corporate services—transforming complex regulatory challenges into strategic advantages that accelerate sustainable growth.

Our Vision

To be the undisputed premier corporate services and transaction advisory partner in Sri Lanka, recognized globally for unwavering integrity, precision, and transformative client success.
