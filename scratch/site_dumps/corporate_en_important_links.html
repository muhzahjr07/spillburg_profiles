Important Links

Why these resources matter

Navigating the Sri Lankan regulatory and business landscape requires access to authoritative, up-to-date information. This curated directory links you directly to the institutions, authorities, and publications that matter most to businesses operating in or entering Sri Lanka.

What you will find here
  • Government portals for company registration and statutory compliance
  • Regulatory authorities for banking, securities, and insurance
  • Taxation authorities and e-filing systems
  • Arbitration bodies and commercial dispute resolution institutions
  • Capital markets and stock exchange resources
  • Sri Lankan business and financial news sources

Regulatory & Statutory Authorities

Securities & Exchange Commission (SEC) of Sri Lanka

Capital markets regulator overseeing public company disclosures, broker-dealer licensing, insider trading enforcement, and IPO approvals.

Insurance Regulatory Commission (IRCSL)

Sri Lanka Accounting & Auditing Standards Monitoring Board (SLAASMB)

Monitors compliance with Sri Lanka Accounting Standards (SLFRS/LKAS) and Sri Lanka Auditing Standards by listed and specified entities.

Sri Lanka Customs
