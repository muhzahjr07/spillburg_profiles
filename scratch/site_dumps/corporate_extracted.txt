About Us
The Spillburg Story
Specialist services for investors in Sri Lanka.
Spillburg Corporate Services is a limited liability company, having its primary objective to offer specialised services for potential investors to set-up operations in Sri Lanka.
Spillburg is managed by a team of professionals having wide experience in the fields of Corporate Management, Marketing, Legal, Accounting, Taxation, Administration and Logistics.
We provide a One-Stop-Shop
We provide One-Stop-Shop solutions to our esteemed clients — advising on all aspects of carrying out businesses and supporting investors in Sri Lanka. Our main objective is to provide value-for-money services through a thorough understanding of each venture, timely advice and practical solutions to accelerate growth.
We Have a Proven Track Record
Spillburg Corporate Services has successfully facilitated many visas for numerous clients in double quick time. We maintain strong relationships with the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka and other government agencies, including the Customs Department, Department of Immigration and Emigration, Registrar of Companies and Department of Inland Revenue, as well as private institutions and business chambers.
