Due Diligence (DD)

Service overview

Make high-confidence decisions. Our multi-disciplinary Due Diligence team combines financial forensics, legal analysis, and sector intelligence to provide a complete, unbiased picture of any target business or asset in Sri Lanka and the region.

Spillburg's Due Diligence practice delivers independent, forensic-grade investigations that give investors, acquirers, and boards complete visibility into counterparty risk before capital is committed. We examine every layer of a target — financials, operations, regulatory standing, and management — to surface material issues and quantify risk accurately.

  • Financial Due Diligence
  • Legal & Regulatory Due Diligence
  • Operational Due Diligence
  • Tax Due Diligence
  • Commercial Due Diligence
  • Vendor Due Diligence
  • Red Flag Reviews
Delivery outputs

  • Detailed financial quality-of-earnings and working capital analysis
  • Legal compliance and contractual obligation mapping
  • Tax exposure assessment and contingent liability quantification
