About Us
Who We Are?
Spillburg Logistics 1 (Private) Limited is a Logistic Service Provider with a team of experts in providing international transport solutions for customers around the globe. We deliver reliable cargo handling services of both export and import consignments at customs in Sri Lanka. Ensuring that cargo arrives at the right place and at the right time involves real skill. Spillburg Logistics 1 (Private) Limited ensures that the moving of goods from one part of the world to another is done economically and efficiently. Proper procedure and documentation are followed to facilitate the transaction process.
Fast & Professional
Moving products, raw materials and equipment in any place worldwide requires expertise and this is why when it comes to moving, businesses rely on freight forwarding companies. Ask any kind of business that requires freight services and they will tell you how important relying on an experienced and efficient freight forwarding company is. With Spillburg Logistics that will indeed be guaranteed.
Over Customized Solutions
Spillburg Logistics 1 starts with a complete assessment of your business needs. By doing so no matter how simple or how complicated your logistics and supplies management needs are you will get exactly the best and the most updated solutions for your company. From the time you talk to us for your orders to the time we deliver the goods to your required destination, you need to have customized solutions that will surely yield excellent results all the time.

Mission
To provide total logistics solutions that gain confidence & trust of our valued clients by conducting business with complete integrity.
