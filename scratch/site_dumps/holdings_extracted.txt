Who We Are
Three decades guiding growth in Sri Lanka.
Spillburg Holdings is a premier business advisory and management consultancy — the trusted bridge between global ambition and the Sri Lankan market.
Our Story
A diversified group built on trust.
Since 1990, we have strategically assisted global investors in establishing, scaling and optimizing their operations across Sri Lanka — combining deep local intelligence with global best practices.
From company formation and regulatory approvals to visa facilitation, legal advisory and elite business-council membership, our diversified expertise lets clients enter and grow with absolute confidence and compliance.
