# Spillburg Group — Design System & Visual Architecture

A unified yet distinct design language tailored for each company vertical within the Spillburg Group.

---

## 1. Destination Consultancy (Pvt) Ltd
*Strategic Investment & Cross-Border Advisory*

- **Visual Style**: Premium Advisory & Sovereign Wealth Aesthetic
- **Color Palette**:
  - Deep Petrol: `#071E2B`
  - Sovereign Gold: `#C59B27`
  - Ivory Cream: `#FAF7F2`
  - Card Glass: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`
- **Typography**:
  - Primary / Body: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  - Accents / Numbers: `Outfit`
- **Key Visual Elements**: Compass monogram emblem, gold borders, 4-director leadership grid, Sri Lanka 5-Hub strategic cards.

---

## 2. Spillburg Corporate Services (Pvt) Ltd
*Governance, Secretarial, Tax & Audit Practice*

- **Visual Style**: Swiss Legal-Editorial & Big-4 Advisory
- **Color Palette**:
  - Royal Midnight: `#030712`
  - Electric Cobalt: `#173DED`
  - Deep Indigo: `#00004D`
  - Alabaster Gray: `#F3F4F6`
- **Typography**:
  - Headings & Editorial Display: `Cormorant Garamond` (Serif)
  - Body & Practice Text: `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  - Metric / Telemetry Accents: `JetBrains Mono`
- **Key Visual Elements**: Asymmetric split-cover, right-side glassmorphic practice matrix, top-accented architectural cards, trusted client logo grid.

## 3. Spillburg Logistics 1 (Pvt) Ltd
